A plain checkerboard: 22 columns, 14 rows, every other square filled.
Then the columns get narrower toward a fold, squeezed by up to 90%, and the flat grid seems to bend away from you.
Movement in Squares, after Bridget Riley's painting from 1961. An interpretation, not the original.
Slide the fold and the bend moves with it.
