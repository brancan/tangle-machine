How much disorder does a perfect grid need?
Vera Molnár called the idea "1% de désordre."
Here, every corner of every square moves by a share of the gap between squares.
It settles to perfect order, then 1%: barely visible. At 150% the grid comes apart.
Same seed, same instruction, only the disorder changes.
