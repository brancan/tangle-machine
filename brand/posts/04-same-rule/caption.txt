Same rule, five sets of numbers.
A harmonograph is two swinging pendulums steering one pen. As the swing dies away, the line winds inward into a knot.
The instruction never changes: only how often each pendulum swings, how far out of step, how fast it fades.
Two against three, one against two, in unison, five against four, five against six.
