One instruction. Your browser holds the pencil.
Sol LeWitt wrote his wall drawings as instructions and let draughtsmen carry them out.
Here the instruction is a function: a 4 × 4 grid, lines in four directions, a random combination in each square.
The reel shows it drawn line by line, square by square, in the order the code draws them. Then the seed changes: same words, different random choices.
An interpretation in code, not LeWitt's original.
