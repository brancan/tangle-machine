Same drawing, same seed. Only the hand shakes.
A machine draws a perfectly straight line. A hand doesn't.
Every drawing on the site has four hand-drawn sliders: wobble bends the lines, jitter moves their ends, pressure changes their weight, roughness frays their edges.
Here they rise from 0 to their maximum over a Wall Drawing after Sol LeWitt (an interpretation in code, not his original). The squares stay the same; the hand gets less steady.
